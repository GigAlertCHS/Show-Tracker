If you sign in, we store your email address, My Shows list, and Favorite Artists so they sync across your devices and power the weekly digest email. Venue suggestions you submit are stored along with your email so we can follow up if needed.

Digest emails include open and click tracking (via our email provider, Resend) so we can see whether emails are opened and which links get clicked — this is to understand what's actually useful, not to build profiles on anyone. We don't sell or share your information with advertisers or any other third party.

Site hosting, backend storage, and email delivery are handled by GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, and Resend respectively, each under their own privacy practices.

Unsubscribe from emails anytime via the link in any digest — once you do, signing back in won't automatically re-subscribe you; use the "Subscribe to email updates" link above if you change your mind. To ask us to delete your data entirely, email admin@gigalertchs.com.
